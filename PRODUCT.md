# RMPH Consignment Inventory Management System (HECS)

## Overview
The Roxas Memorial Provincial Hospital (RMPH) Consignment System manages high-value medical supplies, implants, orthopedic kits, and consignment pharmaceuticals under the Capiz Provincial Government healthcare jurisdiction.

## Core Workflows
1. **Clinical Ward Requisition Portal**: Fast, accessible bedside requesting for nurses and department in-charges with schedule enforcement and life-safety emergency overrides.
2. **Consignment Operations Center**: Multi-department inventory tracking partitioning **Pharmacy** and **Medical Supplies**.
3. **Automated FEFO Allocation**: First-Expired, First-Out lot and batch dispatching ensuring supplies closest to expiration are issued first.
4. **Perpetual Ledger & Valuation Audit**: Instant stockcards, bin cards, and Appendix 47 Purchase Requests conforming to provincial and hospital audit mandates.

## Target Personas
- **Chief of Hospital II & Administrators**: System-wide governance, audit sign-offs, and liability ledger monitoring.
- **Consignment Officers (Pharmacy & Med Supplies)**: Delivery reception (DR), batch lot tracking, stock adjustments, and supplier reconciliations.
- **Ward In-Charge / Nurses (CSR, HDU, OR, ER)**: Rapid material requisition, emergency slips, and stock issue confirmations.
